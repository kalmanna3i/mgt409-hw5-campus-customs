# Desk design

The page is a shop counter, not a default app shell. The background is paper tan, the header is Yale blue, and the checking balance sits in a dark green cash box so the money is the first thing a person sees on the right.

Tickets are three paper stubs in a row. The selected stub gets a blue outline. Resolved status is green. A ticket that still needs a human pay shows “needs approval” in red on the stub.

Under the stubs, the left column is the work order: who asked, the sku, Run the team, the human’s name, and Approve pay with the dollar amount on the button. The right side is one strip per agent. Boss is gold, Inventory is green, Accounting is blue, Facilities is brown, and Customer Service is brick red, so you can tell who is talking without reading the label twice.

After a payment, the green cash box shows the new checking balance. A resolved ticket keeps the outcome note on the work order so the desk still explains what happened after the run ends.

The narrow layout stacks the stubs and the agent strips, so the same desk is usable on a phone.
