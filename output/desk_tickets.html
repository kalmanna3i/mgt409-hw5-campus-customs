Reflection

How I would grade the agents on each ticket

Ticket 101 did the useful thing. Inventory did not invent a size S tee, Accounting noticed invoice 501 was due 2026-08-28 against shop today 2026-08-31, and cash stayed at $3400 until Kay approved $840. I would score that well because the human gate held and the customer draft admits the shirt is still qty 0 with a 5-day lead time.

Ticket 102 also did the useful thing. Facilities, not Inventory, owned the lease, the rent matched $2400, and the pay waited until checking had $2560 left. After approval the lease next_due moved to 2026-10-02. That is the right table update for a real rent payment.

Ticket 103 is the one I would watch. The quote of $49.30 for 8 hoodies stays above the $22 cost, and refusing $264 when checking was $160 is correct. The team did not call Facilities, which matches the plan. It resolved without a sale, which is honest because this homework never adds revenue.

Actual compared with Expected

Ticket 101 matched the plan: Inventory first, then the unpaid invoice, then a human $840 approval, then a customer draft. Actual also recorded the balance moving from $3400 to $2560.

Ticket 102 matched the plan: Facilities first, rent of $2400 due in 2 days, human approval, no inventory agent. Actual added the new due date 2026-10-02, which the expected note did not spell out but the lease tool required.

Ticket 103 matched the plan: Inventory first, shortfall 12, Accounting refuses the restock, Customer Service drafts the 8-unit offer. One difference: by the time this ticket ran, invoice 501 was already paid, so the vendor block was gone and the refusal was the $160 cash balance, not an open bill.

What would have been simpler as one agent

Ticket 103’s discount math is a single worksheet: qty on hand, list, cost, and cash. One agent with the same MCP tools could have quoted $49.30 and refused $264 without a handoff. The split still helped on 101 and 102, because the person who counts shirts should not be the person who moves rent.

Three problems this team could take with the tools it has

Another size of the white tee is in stock (M has 5). Inventory can read that row and Customer Service can offer it without a new tool.

A second rent month could be prepared from the lease row, and Accounting can refuse it if checking is still $160.

The crest mug is qty 0. Inventory can point at Elm City Gifts, and vendor_open_invoices can say whether that vendor will ship.

Three problems this team cannot take yet

A customer payment for the hoodie quote. Cash only leaves, and there is no tool to record money coming in. We would need a receive-payment tool and an orders table.

A real email to Tauhid Zaman or Yale AI Club. Customer Service is only allowed to leave a draft. We would need a send tool with a human confirm step.

Choosing QuickShip CT for a rush once the reprint exists. Nothing in the tools books a courier or spends their 1-day lead time. We would need a shipment tool and a Facilities or Inventory step that writes a delivery row.
